Privacy Policy

Wiz SnapClean by Wiz Labs · Effective 20 November 2026

Summary

Wiz SnapClean has no accounts and does not upload your screenshots or any photos. It looks only at screenshots on your phone, shows you a preview, and deletes or keeps them as you choose. Everything it knows stays on your phone.

What the app does

When Android saves a new image, the system wakes SnapClean once. The app checks whether the image is a screenshot (its folder or file name contains "screenshot"), shows a notification with a preview, and, if you choose a timer, moves the screenshot to your phone's Trash (or deletes it, if you pick that option) when the timer ends. Pictures that are not screenshots are never opened, listed or changed. The app has no always-running service.

Permissions

  • Photos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, or storage on older Android): to notice new screenshots, show their preview and move them to Trash. Android 14 lets you share only selected photos; in that mode SnapClean cannot see new screenshots.
  • Media management (MANAGE_MEDIA), optional: lets timers move screenshots to Trash without a confirmation dialog each time. You can turn it off in Android Settings at any time. Without it, Android asks you once for the whole batch.
  • Notifications: to ask what to do with each screenshot.
  • Run after restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to start watching again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet access: used only by Google Play for app updates and review prompts. SnapClean itself sends nothing about your screenshots anywhere. This version shows no ads.

What we collect

  • Screenshot images and previews: never uploaded, never sent to Wiz Labs or anyone else. Previews are generated on your phone and kept in memory only. On the lock screen, the preview follows Android's "hide sensitive content" setting.
  • Copy to clipboard (on by default): SnapClean keeps one private copy of your newest screenshot in its own app cache so you can paste it. It never leaves your phone, is replaced by the next screenshot, and is deleted, together with the clipboard entry, when SnapClean cleans screenshots. Turn it off in Settings.
  • On-device records: a small list on your phone with each handled screenshot's ID, size, folder, time and chosen timer, plus your settings and running totals (screenshots cleaned, space freed). Records of cleaned screenshots are removed after two weeks. Android may back up your settings, never the screenshot list.
  • Analytics, accounts, advertising IDs: none.

Third-party components

The app includes Google Play services libraries for in-app updates and review prompts, and a Google ad library that is switched off in this version and shows no ads. These may process technical data such as device and diagnostic information under Google's privacy policy. If ads are added in a future version, this policy and the in-app consent form will be updated first.

Your choices

Pause or turn off watching from the app or the Quick Settings tile, change or remove permissions in Android Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all its data. Items moved to Trash stay there for about 30 days (Android 11 and up) and can be restored from your gallery or Files app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the app's GitHub page or use the developer contact on Google Play.
